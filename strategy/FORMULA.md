# The Top Five Facts formula (DRAFT — waiting for approval)

Built from: vidIQ research (`RESEARCH.md`) + Shorts Council review of Country Series Ep. 1 (Japan).
Once approved, every video follows these rules.

## Length & pace
- **18–22 seconds total.** (Breakout travel/fact top-5s run 15–25 s.)
- Hook ≤ 2 s · each place ~2.5 s · guess ~1.5 s · #1 ~3 s · close ~2.5 s.
- **A new shot every 1.5–2 s.** 2–3 shots per place (different photos/angles or crops), hard cuts or whip/zoom transitions on each "Number X" beat — no long slow fades.
- Stronger camera movement (zoom 1.0 → 1.15 or a visible pan); never a still frame longer than 2 s.

## Hook (first 2 seconds)
- **Frame 0 = the best visual** (usually #1's most striking shot, or a fast cut through all 5) with **huge text** (country name ~260 px) on a dark gradient.
- Spoken hook makes a specific promise or tension, e.g. "5 places in Japan you can't skip. Number one is unreal."
- Title words that break out: **"Most Beautiful"**, flag emoji, curiosity ("#1 is unreal", "Save this").

## Text & layout
- **Minimum text size 48 px**; all key text between **15 % and 65 % of the height** (the bottom is covered by YouTube's caption/buttons).
- High contrast (white/solid on dark scrim). No tiny headers.
- **One consistent colour grade** for every shot: warm, a bit more contrast and saturation — never grey/washed out.

## Script & voice
- **~55–60 words** for ~20 s. Name the exact spot in every line ("Kyoto's Fushimi Inari", "Osaka's Dotonbori").
- The photo must show exactly what the line says, and **cut on the place name**.
- Numbers written out in words for the voice. No hint that gives away the next line.
- ElevenLabs: speed 1.10–1.15, stability 0.35–0.45, similarity 0.75, style 0.30–0.40, speaker boost on; same settings every episode.

## Sound
- Voice first, but **not silent**: a low music bed (well under the voice) + subtle whooshes/hits on each rank. (Pure voice felt "dead, like a slideshow".)

## Ending, CTA & series
- **Like/Subscribe overlay for ~1 s during #1**, not after it.
- Spoken close: like prompt + "Follow, [next country]'s next." On-screen card must **match the words** and be large and centred.
- Last line flows back into the first so the replay loops.
- Pick a **less predictable #1 or add a surprising fact** to the #1 reveal (Fuji alone felt obvious).
- Series name in description/playlist, **not "Ep. 1" in the title**. Caption + pinned comment: "Save this for your trip ✈️" and "Which country next? 👇".
