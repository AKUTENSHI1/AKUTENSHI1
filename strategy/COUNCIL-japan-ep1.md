# Shorts Council — Country Series Ep. 1 (Japan), 2026-11-15

Scores: Video editor 4/10 · Shorts strategist 5/10 · Typical viewer 4/10 · Script & voice coach 5/10.
Consensus: **premium look, but too long, too slow, too quiet — plays like a slideshow.**

## Where all four agreed (strongest signal)
1. **Too long:** 34 s vs 15–25 s for breakout travel Shorts. Each place ~5 s on one still shot.
2. **Weak first second:** small, low-contrast text on the same Osaka street that comes next; generic "Going to Japan?".
3. **Dead zones:** the grey "Can you guess No. 01?" card and ~8 s of the same grey Fuji shot at the end.
4. **No energy:** calm voice + no music + slow fades = viewers swipe (predicted around 9–12 s).

## Other points
- Text too small and too low (bottom of the screen is covered by YouTube's UI).
- Colour grade inconsistent; Fuji washed-out grey.
- Narration doesn't name exact spots (Fushimi Inari, Dotonbori); opening reuses Osaka.
- On-screen "Have you been to Japan?" doesn't match the spoken "Follow, next is Italy".
- Fuji at #1 is predictable — needs a surprising fact or twist.
- "Ep. 1" in the title gives strangers no reason to click.

## Proposed script (~20 s, coach)
Five places in Japan you can't skip. Number one is unreal. /
Five: Osaka's Dotonbori, Japan's street-food capital. /
Four: Nara Park, where over a thousand deer roam free. /
Three: Tokyo's Shibuya Crossing, one of the busiest on Earth. /
Two: Kyoto's Fushimi Inari, thousands of red torii gates. /
Number one? Guess fast. /
Mount Fuji. Three thousand seven hundred seventy-six meters. /
Like if you'd go. Follow, Italy's next.
