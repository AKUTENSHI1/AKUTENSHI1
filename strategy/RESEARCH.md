## Patterns
1. **Tiny channels can explode.** Channels with 600–4,000 subscribers got 100K–4.6M views.
   One great Short is enough; channel size barely matters.
2. **Travel Top-5s that break out are short: ~15–25 s** (Greece 0:21, Tamil Nadu 0:15,
   Northeast India 0:22). Longer travel Shorts (0:49–1:16) win when they promise
   something useful ("route", "save this", "tourist traps").
3. **Titles are short, emotional, and use the flag emoji.** "Top 5 most beautiful places in
   Greece 🇬🇷" beats a generic "places to visit". Curiosity or warning words work:
   "DO NOT", "Unreal", "Sound fake", "Wait for the last one", "only in Japan".
4. **"Most beautiful" framing** appears more in breakout titles than "places to visit".
5. **Negative / danger framing earns the highest engagement** in facts ("DEADLIEST
   planets" 7.2% engagement vs ~1–3% typical).
6. **Space facts with a story/voice can run longer (1–3 min)** and still break out; pure
   list montages stay short.
7. **"Which country…?" comparison and "facts that sound fake"** are proven repeatable series
   formats.

## What this means for us
- Country Series: aim for **~20 s** (our Japan Ep. 1 is 34 s — too long for this format).
  A faster voice and a tighter script are the biggest win.
- Titles: **"Top 5 most beautiful places in Japan 🇯🇵"**-style, plus a curiosity hook.
- Keep the "Save this for your trip" angle in the hook or caption: it drives saves/shares.
- Space/facts: lean into **danger, "sounds fake", and "wait for the last one"** angles.
