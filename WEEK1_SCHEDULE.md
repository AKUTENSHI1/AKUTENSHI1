## Sources for the numbers
- Deserts: total area including polar deserts (Antarctic, Arctic).
- Planets: equatorial diameter (NASA).
- Countries: total area including water (CIA World Factbook ranking).
- Buildings: height to architectural top (CTBUH), as of 2026.
- Islands: Australia is a continent, so it is not counted.
- Moons: mean diameter (NASA). Ganymede and Titan are both wider than Mercury (4,879 km).
- Population: UN World Population Prospects estimates for 2025. India passed China in 2023.
