## 3. Voiceover script (exact)
> Five ways the world could end, and how you'd survive each one.
> Number five. Asteroid. NASA already proved it can nudge one off course, so your job is simple: follow evacuation orders.
> Number four. Supervolcano. Ash blocks sunlight and crops fail. Store two weeks of food and water, and keep masks for the ash.
> Number three. Pandemic. Wash your hands, stay home when sick, and stay current on vaccines.
> Number two. Solar superstorm. It can take down power grids. Keep batteries, cash, and a paper map.
> Number one. Nuclear war. Get inside. Stay inside. Stay tuned.
> Whatever it is, the plan is the same: water, food, a radio, and a plan. Save this.
